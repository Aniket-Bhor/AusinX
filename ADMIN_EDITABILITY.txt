  * Missing: Icon selection (fixed to iconMap based on slug), Card guarantee badges ("Immediate Engineer Access", etc.).

- Home Hero Section:
  * Editable: Stats Bar numbers, labels, descriptions, and toggle. Hero Image upload.
  * Missing: Hero main headline, subtitle copy, and CTA button labels/targets.

- Bento Grid Section:
  * Editable: 4 Modality slot images (bentoCt, bentoCArm, bentoMonitor, bentoUltrasound).
  * Missing: Modality titles, descriptions, categories, and feature pills.

- Why Choose Us Section:
  * Editable: 4 Value proposition point titles and descriptions. Image upload slot.
  * Missing: Section heading, intro text, and image overlay badge.

- Header & Footer:
  * Editable: Logo image, dynamic service links, phone numbers, email address, physical address.
  * Missing: Nav links menu (About, Contact, Home), footer blurb, copyright statement, developer credit link, social media links.

- Contact Page:
  * Editable: Phone lines, email, address, business hours, service dropdown options, side image.
  * Missing: Form field labels, validation messages, form confirmation message, page title and intro copy.

- Site Settings & Branding:
  * Editable: Company Name, Tagline, Public Email, Street Address, Phone list, Business Hours, Header Logo, Color Palette (12 themes).
  * Missing: Social media links (LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook), Google Maps embed URL / coordinates, Favicon / Apple touch icon upload.

4. SUGGESTED ADDITIONS RANKED BY CLIENT VALUE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Global & Page-Level SEO Metadata Editor: Allow editing page titles, meta descriptions, and OG social share cards for Home, About, Contact, and Services.
2. Home Hero Headline & CTA Customizer: Bind the hero headline, subtitle, and CTA button text/links to admin siteSettings so marketing campaigns can be updated anytime.
3. Privacy Policy & Legal Terms Editor: A WYSIWYG or markdown editor in Admin to customize and review the draft Privacy Policy without code deploys.
4. Social Media & External Links Manager: Admin inputs for LinkedIn company page, Twitter, and custom footer links.
5. Footer Blurb & Copyright Customizer: Let the client edit the footer company overview paragraph and copyright statement.
6. Interactive Bento Modalities Manager: Allow custom titles, categories, and descriptive pills for the 4 homepage equipment showcase cards.
7. About Page Mission & Capabilities Editor: Allow editing the Mission/Values cards and Modality Capabilities on the About page.
8. Favicon & Web App Icon Uploader: Allow uploading custom browser favicon and apple-touch-icon alongside the existing header logo editor.
9. Contact Form Email Recipients & Auto-Responder: Specify which staff email receives website enquiry notifications and customize the user confirmation message.
10. Operating Hours & Emergency Line Settings: Configurable 24/7 emergency service line toggle and weekday business hours display in Header/Footer.
