================================================================================
           AUSINX WEBSITE - CLIENT EDITABILITY GUIDE & AUDIT
================================================================================

This document gives you a clear, plain-language breakdown of what you can currently
edit from your Admin Panel, what is currently fixed in the code, and recommended
additions.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT YOU CAN EDIT RIGHT NOW (BY ADMIN TAB)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[Enquiries]
- View, filter, review, and archive customer messages sent from the Contact form.

[Home Page]
- Hero Stats Bar: Turn on/off the numbers strip below the main banner (e.g. 24/7, 99.8%).
- Hero Stats Numbers & Labels: Edit the statistic, title, and description for each metric.
- 5-Stage Lifecycle Workflow: Edit titles and descriptions for each step of the process.
- Why Choose Us Points: Edit the 4 value points explaining why clinics trust AusinX.

[Services]
- Add new services or remove existing ones.
- Service Titles, Short Summaries, and Detailed Descriptions.
- Service Scope & Capabilities: Add, reorder, or edit sub-services with checkmarks.
- Operational Commitments: Edit the key highlight bullet points.
- Service Images: Upload custom photos for each specific service page.
- URL Slugs & Web Addresses (e.g. /services/service-maintenance).

[Images]
- Header & Footer Brand Logo (upload image or use built-in vector logo).
- Homepage Hero Banner Image.
- 4 Equipment Showcase Photos (CT Systems, C-Arms, Patient Monitors, Ultrasound).
- Feature Photo on the "Why Choose Us" section.
- About Us Page Workshop Photo.
- Contact Us Page Support Desk Photo.

[Colors & Theme]
- Switch between 12 distinct professionally designed color palettes (Emerald, Cobalt, 
  Slate, Indigo, Terracotta, Crimson, Golden Bronze, Rosewood, Amethyst, Graphite, 
  Olive Grove, Deep Aqua).
- Instantly changes header tints, buttons, badges, and accents across the whole site.
- One-click "Reset to Original" button.

[Business Info]
- Official Company Name (appears in header, footer, and copyright).
- Company Tagline.
- Public Contact Email.
- Primary Phone Number (used for one-click calling buttons).
- Secondary Phone Number.
- Operating / Business Hours.
- Physical Office Address (Street, City, State, Postcode).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHAT IS CURRENTLY FIXED / CANNOT BE EDITED IN ADMIN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The following text and elements are currently hardcoded in the website template.
To change them, a quick update in the code file is required:

* Header:
  - Top navigation menu labels ("Home", "Services", "About Us").
  - "Talk to our team" button text and its target link (/contact).

* Home Page:
  - Main hero headline ("High-Performance Industrial & Biomedical Engineering").
  - Hero sub-paragraph below the main headline.
  - Hero action buttons ("Talk to our team", "Explore Modalities").
  - "OEM-Trained Engineers" and "Under 2-Hour Response" trust badges.
  - Modality titles and feature pills on the 4 equipment showcase cards (the photos 
    ARE editable, but the card text is fixed).

* About Us Page:
  - Main headline and introduction story.
  - "Our Mission: Zero Downtime", "Technical Transparency", and "End-to-End Accountability" cards.
  - "Technical Standards & Modality Capabilities" 4 cards.
  - "How AusinX Delivers Engineering Certainty" 4 standard cards.

* Contact Us Page:
  - Page title and introductory sentence.
  - Enquiry form field labels ("Full Name", "Work Email", "Message", etc.).

* Privacy Policy Page:
  - The complete legal privacy policy text and sections.

* Footer:
  - Short company introductory paragraph.
  - Copyright line and developer credit link.

* Search Engine Optimization (SEO):
  - Page meta titles and descriptions shown on Google search results.
  - Social media share preview cards (OpenGraph / Twitter).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. TOP 5 RECOMMENDED ADDITIONS FOR CLIENT CONVENIENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Homepage Hero Headline & Subtitle Editor:
   - Edit the big homepage headline and introductory text directly from the Home Page tab.

2. SEO & Google Search Preview Manager:
   - Edit page titles, search descriptions, and social share cards for each page without code.

3. Privacy Policy & Legal Terms Editor:
   - Easily update company legal policies, terms, or compliance notices from the admin panel.

4. Social Media Links:
   - Add fields in "Business Info" for LinkedIn, Twitter, or Facebook profiles to display in footer.

5. Footer Text & Copyright Editor:
   - Customize the footer description and copyright notice at any time.
